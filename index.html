FAQ

Good to know

Which consultation do I need?

Choose the estimate consultation if you already have an idea and want a clear cost. Choose the design and estimate consultation if you need us to create the best and most efficient design for you, with your estimate included.

Do you travel outside Abuja?

Yes. We travel to your site from Lugbe, Abuja. For sites outside Abuja, a travel cost for the trip is added to your price.

Is the fee deducted from my project?

Yes. If you go ahead with us, your consultation fee is deducted from the project cost.

Which days can I book?

Monday to Saturday. Sundays are closed, and days already taken are marked in red.
